# RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

**RIVERBEND COMMONS APARTMENTS**
2140 Sagamore Parkway West, West Lafayette, Indiana 47906

---

**THIS RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT** (the "Lease") is made and entered into this 11th day of March, 2024, by and between **RIVERBEND COMMONS PROPERTY GROUP, LLC**, an Indiana limited liability company ("Landlord"), and the individuals identified in Section 1.2 below (individually and collectively, "Tenant").

## ARTICLE 1 — PREMISES AND PARTIES

**1.1 Premises.** Landlord leases to Tenant the dwelling unit designated as **Unit 4B**, containing four (4) bedrooms and two (2) bathrooms, located in the building commonly known as Riverbend Commons Apartments (the "Premises"), together with a non-exclusive license to use the common areas, in accordance with the terms set forth herein.

**1.2 Tenant.** The following individuals shall constitute Tenant under this Lease: Bedroom A occupant, Bedroom B occupant, Bedroom C occupant, and Bedroom D occupant, each of whom has executed this Lease below.

**1.3 Occupancy Limit.** The Premises shall be occupied by no more than four (4) persons. No person not named in Section 1.2 may reside at the Premises for more than seven (7) consecutive days or fourteen (14) aggregate days in any calendar month without Landlord's prior written consent.

---

## ARTICLE 2 — TERM

**2.1 Initial Term.** The initial term of this Lease shall commence at 12:00 p.m. on **August 1, 2024** and shall terminate at 12:00 p.m. on **July 31, 2025** (the "Initial Term").

**2.2 Possession.** Landlord shall not be liable for failure to deliver possession of the Premises on the commencement date where such failure arises from the holdover of a prior occupant, construction delay, or casualty, and Tenant's sole remedy in such event shall be an abatement of rent prorated for each day possession is withheld.

**2.3 Automatic Renewal.** Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Lease shall **automatically renew for a successive term of twelve (12) months** upon the same terms and conditions except as to rent, which shall be adjusted to the then-prevailing market rate as determined by Landlord in its sole discretion, unless Tenant delivers written notice of non-renewal to Landlord's management office **not less than ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term**. Notice delivered by electronic mail shall not constitute valid notice hereunder. Tenant's failure to deliver timely notice shall be deemed an affirmative election to renew.

---

## ARTICLE 3 — RENT

**3.1 Base Rent.** Tenant shall pay to Landlord as base rent the sum of **Two Thousand Six Hundred Eighty and 00/100 Dollars ($2,680.00) per month**, payable in advance on the first (1st) day of each month without demand, deduction, or setoff.

**3.2 Allocation.** For administrative convenience only, Landlord may apportion base rent among the individuals comprising Tenant at the rate of **Six Hundred Seventy and 00/100 Dollars ($670.00) per bedroom per month**. Such apportionment is for accounting purposes only and shall not operate to limit, divide, or otherwise affect the liability set forth in Section 3.4.

**3.3 Method of Payment.** All payments shall be made through Landlord's designated online portal. Landlord may assess a convenience fee of Twenty-Five and 00/100 Dollars ($25.00) per transaction for payment by any other method.

**3.4 Joint and Several Liability.** Each individual comprising Tenant shall be **jointly and severally liable** for the full and complete performance of each and every obligation of Tenant under this Lease, including without limitation the payment of the entire monthly base rent, all additional rent, all charges, and all damages. Landlord may, at its sole election, proceed against any one or more individuals comprising Tenant for the entire amount owed without first proceeding against, joining, or exhausting remedies against any other individual. The departure, default, dismissal, withdrawal, or death of any individual comprising Tenant shall not reduce the obligations of the remaining individuals.

## ARTICLE 4 — ADDITIONAL RENT AND CHARGES

**4.1 Utilities.** Tenant shall be responsible for electricity, gas, and internet service. Landlord shall provide water, sewer, and trash removal and shall bill Tenant monthly for the same on a per-occupant ratio utility billing basis.

**4.2 Amenity Fee.** Tenant shall pay a non-refundable amenity fee of Forty and 00/100 Dollars ($40.00) per month as additional rent.

**4.3 Late Charges.** If any installment of base rent is not received in full by 11:59 p.m. on the **fifth (5th) day** of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of **Seventy-Five and 00/100 Dollars ($75.00)**, plus an additional **Fifteen and 00/100 Dollars ($15.00) for each day thereafter** that such installment remains unpaid, until paid in full. Late charges shall constitute additional rent. Landlord's acceptance of a partial payment shall not waive any late charge or any other remedy.

**4.4 Returned Payment.** Any payment returned for insufficient funds shall incur a charge of Fifty and 00/100 Dollars ($50.00), and Landlord may thereafter require all payments to be made by certified funds.

## ARTICLE 5 — SECURITY DEPOSIT

**5.1 Amount.** Upon execution of this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of **One Thousand Three Hundred Forty and 00/100 Dollars ($1,340.00)** as security for Tenant's faithful performance of this Lease (the "Security Deposit"). The Security Deposit shall not be construed as prepaid rent and shall not bear interest.

**5.2 Standard Turnover Deductions.** Tenant acknowledges and agrees that upon vacating the Premises, the following charges shall be assessed against the Security Deposit **in all cases and without regard to the condition of the Premises**: (a) professional carpet replacement in each bedroom occupied, at the rate of Two Hundred Twenty-Five and 00/100 Dollars ($225.00) per bedroom; (b) professional cleaning of the Premises at the rate of Three Hundred Fifty and 00/100 Dollars ($350.00); and (c) repainting of all interior walls at the rate of Two Hundred and 00/100 Dollars ($200.00). Tenant waives any claim that such charges constitute ordinary wear and tear.

**5.3 Return.** Any balance of the Security Deposit remaining after the deductions permitted herein shall be returned to Tenant at the forwarding address provided by Tenant in writing.

## ARTICLE 6 — USE OF PREMISES

**6.1 Residential Use.** The Premises shall be used solely as a private residence and for no other purpose. No trade, business, or commercial activity of any kind shall be conducted at the Premises.

**6.2 Quiet Enjoyment.** Tenant shall not permit any conduct that disturbs the quiet enjoyment of other residents. Landlord's determination as to whether conduct is disturbing shall be final.

**6.3 Smoking.** The Premises and all common areas are non-smoking. Violation shall incur a charge of Three Hundred and 00/100 Dollars ($300.00) per occurrence as additional rent.

---

## ARTICLE 7 — PETS

**7.1 Prohibition.** No animal of any kind may be kept at the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent and payment of a non-refundable pet fee of Four Hundred and 00/100 Dollars ($400.00) and pet rent of Forty and 00/100 Dollars ($40.00) per month. This Section shall not apply to assistance animals to the extent required by applicable law.

---

## ARTICLE 8 — ALTERATIONS AND MAINTENANCE BY TENANT

**8.1 No Alterations.** Tenant shall make no alterations, additions, or improvements to the Premises, including painting, wallpapering, or the installation of shelving or mounting hardware, without Landlord's prior written consent.

**8.2 Tenant Maintenance.** Tenant shall keep the Premises in a clean and sanitary condition, shall replace light bulbs, furnace filters, and smoke detector batteries at Tenant's expense, and shall promptly report in writing any condition requiring repair.

---

## ARTICLE 9 — LANDLORD OBLIGATIONS

**9.1 Repairs.** Landlord shall deliver the Premises in a safe, clean, and habitable condition and shall maintain in good and safe working condition the electrical, plumbing, sanitary, heating, ventilating, and air conditioning systems and any appliances supplied by Landlord, and shall comply with all applicable health and housing codes.

**9.2 Notice of Defect.** Tenant shall give Landlord written notice of any condition requiring repair, and Landlord shall have a reasonable time thereafter in which to remedy the same.

---

## ARTICLE 10 — INSURANCE

**10.1 Renter's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain at all times during the term a policy of renter's liability insurance with limits of not less than One Hundred Thousand and 00/100 Dollars ($100,000.00) and shall name Landlord as an additional interested party. Proof of coverage shall be delivered to Landlord prior to occupancy and upon each renewal.

**10.2 Landlord Not Insurer.** Landlord shall not be liable for damage to or loss of Tenant's personal property from any cause whatsoever, including theft, fire, water, or the acts of other residents.

---

## ARTICLE 11 — ACCESS

**11.1 Right of Entry.** Landlord and its agents, employees, and contractors may enter the Premises to inspect, make repairs, perform preventive maintenance, show the Premises to prospective residents, lenders, or purchasers, or for any other reasonable business purpose.

**11.2 Notice of Entry.** Landlord shall provide Tenant such notice of entry as is **practicable under the circumstances**. Tenant acknowledges that notice may be given by posting at the entry door of the Premises and that no minimum advance period is required. No notice shall be required in the event of an emergency, an apparent abandonment, or where entry is made in the ordinary course of scheduled maintenance.

---

## ARTICLE 12 — DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

**12.1 Events of Default.** Tenant shall be in default upon (a) failure to pay any rent or additional rent when due; (b) breach of any other covenant that remains uncured for five (5) days after written notice; or (c) abandonment of the Premises.

**12.2 Acceleration.** Upon default, Landlord may, at its option, declare **the entire balance of rent for the remainder of the term immediately due and payable**, and may pursue all remedies available at law or in equity, including possession.

**12.3 Costs of Enforcement.** Tenant shall pay all costs incurred by Landlord in enforcing this Lease, including reasonable attorney's fees, court costs, and collection agency fees of up to thirty-five percent (35%) of the balance referred.

---

## ARTICLE 13 — SURRENDER

**13.1 Condition on Surrender.** Upon termination, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom-clean, free of all personal property, with all keys, fobs, and parking permits returned to the management office. Any personal property remaining shall be deemed abandoned and may be disposed of by Landlord without liability.

**13.2 Holdover.** Any occupancy after the termination of the term shall constitute a holdover tenancy at a daily rate equal to **two hundred percent (200%)** of the daily equivalent of base rent, plus any consequential damages incurred by Landlord.

---

## ARTICLE 14 — ASSIGNMENT, SUBLETTING, AND RELEASE

**14.1 Prohibition.** Tenant shall **not assign this Lease, sublet the Premises or any portion thereof, or permit occupancy by any person other than an individual named in Section 1.2**, whether voluntarily, involuntarily, or by operation of law. Any purported assignment or sublease shall be void and shall constitute a material default. **Landlord shall be under no obligation to consider, review, or consent to any proposed assignment, sublease, or substitute occupant, and Landlord's refusal to do so shall not be subject to any standard of reasonableness.**

**14.2 No Early Release.** There is no early termination option under this Lease. Tenant's transfer, graduation, withdrawal from an academic institution, loss of employment, study abroad, marriage, military enlistment, or relocation shall not constitute grounds for termination, abatement, or release, except as required by 50 U.S.C. § 3955 (Servicemembers Civil Relief Act).

**14.3 Relet at Landlord's Discretion.** In the event Tenant vacates prior to the end of the term, Landlord may, but shall not be obligated to, attempt to relet the Premises. Any such reletting shall be on terms acceptable to Landlord in its sole discretion, and Tenant shall remain liable for all rent, plus a reletting administration fee of Five Hundred and 00/100 Dollars ($500.00) and any difference between the rent reserved herein and the rent obtained.

## ARTICLE 16 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

**16.1 Governing Law.** This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Indiana. Venue for any action shall lie exclusively in Tippecanoe County, Indiana.

**16.2 Severability.** If any provision of this Lease is held invalid or unenforceable, the remainder shall continue in full force and effect.

**16.3 Entire Agreement.** This Lease, together with the Rules and Regulations, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements, whether written or oral. No representation by any leasing agent shall be binding upon Landlord.

**16.4 Waiver of Landlord Obligations.** To the fullest extent permitted, **Tenant waives and releases Landlord from any and all statutory obligations respecting the condition, repair, and habitability of the Premises**, and waives any right to repair-and-deduct, rent withholding, rent escrow, or the recovery of attorney's fees in connection with any claimed failure by Landlord to maintain the Premises.

**16.5 Waiver of Jury Trial.** The parties hereby waive trial by jury in any action arising out of this Lease.

**16.6 Amendments.** No amendment to this Lease shall be effective unless in writing and signed by Landlord and each individual comprising Tenant.
